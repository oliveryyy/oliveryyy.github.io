YAN, Yongyang (Oliver) 顏永阳

PhD, BSc, RN

Postdoctoral Fellow

School of Nursing, The Hong Kong Polytechnic University

Supervisor: Prof. Jed Ray Gengoba Montayre

Email Scholar GitHub

My research focuses on leveraging mobile health and artificial intelligence to develop theory-driven interventions aimed at managing modifiable risk factors of chronic diseases among stroke patients, such as hypertension and high sodium intake. I also explore the involvement of informal caregivers in dyadic interventions to improve care outcomes.

My work has been translated into international health policy and clinical practice, cited by the World Health Organization, the International Council of Nurses, the American Heart Association, and the American Stroke Association in their reports and statements.
