YAN, Yongyang (Oliver)

顏永阳

Postdoctoral Fellow · PhD, BSc, RN

School of Nursing, The Hong Kong Polytechnic University

Supervisor: Prof. Jed Ray Gengoba Montayre

oliver-yy.yan@polyu.edu.hk Google Scholar

My research focuses on leveraging mobile health and artificial intelligence to develop theory-driven interventions for managing modifiable risk factors of chronic diseases among stroke patients. I also explore informal caregivers' involvement in dyadic interventions and healthy ageing promotion through social prescribing.

My work has been cited by the World Health Organization, the International Council of Nurses, the American Heart Association, and the American Stroke Association in their reports and statements.
