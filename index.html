Dr. YAN, Yongyang (Oliver)

顏永阳

PhD, BSc, RN

Postdoctoral Fellow

School of Nursing, The Hong Kong Polytechnic University

Dr. Yan is a Postdoctoral Fellow at the School of Nursing, The Hong Kong Polytechnic University, supervised by Prof. Jed Ray Gengoba Montayre. He obtained his PhD from The University of Hong Kong as an HKU Presidential PhD Scholarship Awardee and his BSc from Peking University.

His work has been translated into international health policy and clinical practice, cited by the World Health Organization, the International Council of Nurses, the American Heart Association, and the American Stroke Association.

Research Interests

mHealth & AI Interventions

Developing technology-supported behaviour change interventions for chronic disease management in stroke survivors

Dyadic Interventions

Exploring the involvement of informal caregivers in interventions to improve care outcomes for patients with cardiovascular diseases

Healthy Ageing

Promoting social connections and support systems among older adults through social prescribing models

Implementation Science

Advancing evidence-based practices by translating research findings into real-world healthcare settings

Male Nurses' Development

Supporting career advancement and identity development of male nurses within the nursing profession
