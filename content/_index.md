      title: Research Interests
      text: |-
        My research focuses on leveraging **mobile health (mHealth)** and **artificial intelligence** to develop theory-driven interventions aimed at managing modifiable risk factors of chronic diseases among stroke patients, such as hypertension and high sodium intake.

        Key research areas include:

        - **mHealth & AI Interventions** — Developing technology-supported behaviour change interventions for chronic disease management in stroke survivors
        - **Dyadic Interventions** — Exploring the involvement of informal caregivers in interventions to improve care outcomes for patients with cardiovascular diseases
        - **Healthy Ageing** — Promoting social connections and support systems among older adults through social prescribing models
        - **Implementation Science** — Advancing evidence-based practices by translating research findings into real-world healthcare settings
        - **Male Nurses' Professional Development** — Supporting career advancement and identity development of male nurses

        My work has been cited by the **World Health Organization**, the **International Council of Nurses**, the **American Heart Association**, and the **American Stroke Association** in their reports and statements.
