Dr. YAN, Yongyang (Oliver) is a Postdoctoral Fellow at the School of Nursing, The Hong Kong Polytechnic University, supervised by Prof. Jed Ray Gengoba Montayre. He obtained his PhD from The University of Hong Kong as an HKU Presidential PhD Scholarship Awardee and his BSc from Peking University.

His research focuses on leveraging mobile health (mHealth) and artificial intelligence to develop theory-driven interventions for managing modifiable risk factors of chronic diseases among stroke patients. He is also dedicated to advancing implementation science, healthy ageing promotion, and supporting male nurses' professional development.

His work has been translated into international health policy and clinical practice, cited by the World Health Organization, the International Council of Nurses, the American Heart Association, and the American Stroke Association.
